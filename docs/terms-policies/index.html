Terms & policies

This page explains how information submitted through the Pixlwrk website is handled, how optional analytics technologies are controlled, and the basic terms that apply when you use the site.

Privacy

When you contact us through the website we may receive your name, email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to respond to the enquiry and manage the resulting business relationship.

Hosting, security and application services may also process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and browser information where this is necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website.

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers that process data on our behalf where required to operate the website, communications or other agreed services. Information is retained only for as long as there is a legitimate business, contractual, legal or security reason to keep it.

You can ask about the personal information held in connection with your enquiry, or request an appropriate correction or deletion, by emailing hello@digipixel.uk.

Cookies and analytics

Technologies that are strictly necessary for the website to function may operate without optional consent. Analytics or advertising technologies are not enabled until the relevant consent has been granted through the website privacy controls.

You can change or withdraw optional consent at any time using the Cookie settings control in the website footer.
