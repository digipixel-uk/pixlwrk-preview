03 / How we work

A considered process. A working result.

Clear decisions at each stage, an understandable scope and opportunities to review real progress. No mystery about what is being built or why.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start with the people, the problem and what success would actually look like.

    Discovery, requirements and existing systems.
  2. 02

    Advise

    We recommend a practical scope and explain the choices, trade-offs and budget.

    Architecture, priorities and a defined plan.
  3. 03

    Build

    Design and development happen together, with working previews and regular feedback.

    Design, implementation and testing.
  4. 04

    Manage

    Launch is planned, checked and supported rather than treated as the finish line.

    Launch, hosting and ongoing care.
  5. 05

    Improve

    We refine what the data and real-world use tell us needs attention.

    Measurement, iteration and next steps.
