Case study / Software platform / Laravel

Flight School Finder A specialist discovery journey built around real decisions.

A flight training discovery and comparison platform, with structured schools and courses, realistic cost planning and a consent-led enquiry journey.

Public website live · application pilot in development
PROJECT / FLIGHT SCHOOL FINDERSearch, comparison and enquiries

01 / The challenge

What needed to change.

Aspiring pilots need to compare very different training routes and costs. A generic directory cannot adequately explain training progression or total costs.

02 / The approach

The thinking behind the build.

Design the research and decision journey first, then develop a Laravel platform with structured provider/course information, comparison, administration and a moderated enquiry workflow.

03 / The process

How the project comes together.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Research the decisions pilots make and the information training providers can reliably supply.

  2. 02

    Advise

    Establish comparison rules, trust principles and a commercial validation plan.

  3. 03

    Build

    Implement the first functional discovery, costing and enquiry flow with a managed provider data model.

  4. 04

    Manage & improve

    Validate the product with prospective students and training providers before live enquiry activation.

04 / Current result

Where it stands.

A functional development-stage platform and a public project website; commercial validation and live enquiry delivery remain ahead.

Project scope

What we delivered

  • Product and journey design
  • Custom Laravel application
  • Provider and course data
  • Search, comparison and enquiry workflows

Features and decisions

Inside the solution

  • Training discovery based on goals, location and experience
  • Provider, course and pricing data structures
  • Inertia/Vue public interface and Filament administration
  • Functional development pilot uses sample data; real school enquiry delivery has not been activated
