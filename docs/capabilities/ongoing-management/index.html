QUESTIONS / ANSWERED

Useful things to know before we start.

Is this the same as a support contract?

It can include support, but it is broader. The emphasis is on ownership and improvement across the digital estate rather than simply responding to faults or small change requests.

Can you coordinate other suppliers?

Yes. Retained management can include coordinating specialist suppliers, internal teams and priorities so the business has one joined-up route rather than separate conversations.

Can retained work start small?

Yes. The right cadence depends on the amount of activity. Some businesses need a light monthly review and backlog; others need more active day-to-day coordination.
