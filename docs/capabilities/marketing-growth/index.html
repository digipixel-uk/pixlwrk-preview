THE DETAIL

Digital marketing that connects attention to a useful business outcome.

SEO, paid search and social media are different channels, but they should not become separate islands. We build the acquisition route around who you need to reach, what they need to hear, where they are likely to respond and what should happen after the click.

01

SEO — search engine optimisation

Improve the visibility and usefulness of the site for the searches that matter, combining technical foundations, content, authority and a realistic understanding of search intent.

  • Technical SEO audits
  • Keyword and search-intent research
  • Service and landing-page optimisation
  • Content planning and internal linking
  • Local SEO where relevant
  • Measurement and continuous improvement
02

PPC — paid search & paid media

Use paid media where it can create a measurable route to demand, rather than treating clicks or impressions as the objective.

  • Google Ads and paid search
  • Campaign and account structure
  • Keyword and audience strategy
  • Ad copy and creative
  • Landing-page alignment
  • Conversion tracking and optimisation
03

SMM — social media marketing

Build a sensible role for social media, whether that is awareness, authority, community, lead generation or paid distribution.

  • Channel and audience planning
  • Organic social content
  • Paid social campaigns
  • Creative formats and content systems
  • Community and response planning
  • Performance review
04

Content marketing

Create useful content that answers real questions, strengthens search visibility and gives campaigns something worth sending people to.

  • Editorial strategy
  • Articles and guides
  • Service-page content
  • Lead magnets and campaign assets
  • Repurposing across channels
05

Landing pages & conversion optimisation

Traffic is only useful if the next step makes sense. We improve the message, page experience and measurement between first visit and enquiry, booking or sale.

  • Dedicated landing pages
  • Offer and message testing
  • Forms and calls to action
  • Analytics and funnel review
  • Conversion-rate optimisation
06

Analytics & marketing measurement

Define what a useful result looks like and make reporting answer commercial questions, not just produce dashboards.

  • GA4 and event measurement
  • Campaign attribution
  • Lead-quality feedback loops
  • Reporting frameworks
  • Monthly optimisation priorities

QUESTIONS / ANSWERED

Useful things to know before we start.

Do you offer SEO as a standalone service?

Yes, when SEO is the right route. A standalone engagement can cover technical SEO, content and page optimisation, or an ongoing programme. We still connect the work to the wider customer journey so rankings are not treated as the final outcome.

Can you manage Google Ads and PPC?

Yes. PixlWrk can plan, build and optimise paid-search campaigns, including tracking and landing-page alignment so spend is judged by useful outcomes rather than traffic alone.

What does social media marketing include?

It can include organic content, paid social, channel planning, campaign creative and performance review. The exact mix depends on whether social is being used for visibility, authority, community, leads or a combination.

Do we have to use every marketing channel?

No. The point is to choose the channels that fit the audience and objective. A focused SEO and PPC route can be better than spreading budget thinly across every available platform.
