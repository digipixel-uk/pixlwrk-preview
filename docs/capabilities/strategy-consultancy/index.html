THE DETAIL

Make the next digital decision with evidence behind it.

Strategy does not have to mean a long report that sits on a shelf. We use discovery, evidence and commercial context to turn a broad digital problem into a practical sequence of decisions, owners and next actions.

01

Digital strategy

A joined-up view of where digital activity should support the business, which audiences matter, what the customer journey needs to do and where investment should happen first.

  • Business and customer objectives
  • Channel and journey priorities
  • Measures of success
  • Practical phased roadmap
02

Digital audits & reviews

An independent review of the current website, marketing activity, content, systems and data so decisions start from what is actually happening rather than assumption.

  • Website and content review
  • Marketing and acquisition review
  • Technology and process review
  • Risk, duplication and opportunity mapping
03

Roadmaps & prioritisation

A realistic route through competing ideas. We separate urgent fixes, high-value opportunities and longer-term improvements, then sequence them around budget and capacity.

  • Impact-versus-effort prioritisation
  • Dependencies and sequencing
  • Budget-aware planning
  • Clear next-step recommendations
04

Independent digital advice

Useful when you need a second opinion before committing to a platform, supplier, campaign, rebuild or internal project.

  • Platform and supplier evaluation
  • Scope and proposal review
  • Build-versus-buy decisions
  • Fractional digital leadership

QUESTIONS / ANSWERED

Useful things to know before we start.

Do we need a full digital strategy project?

Not necessarily. Some problems need a focused review and a short action plan; others justify a broader strategy. The scope should match the decision you need to make.

Can you review work proposed by another supplier?

Yes. PixlWrk can provide an independent view on scope, platform choices, priorities and whether the proposed work addresses the actual business problem.

Can strategy lead into delivery?

Yes. Where it makes sense, the same route can continue into website, marketing, creative, systems or ongoing management work without forcing every capability into the project.

THE IMPORTANT BIT

This is a capability, not a package.

PixlWrk only brings this station into the route when it is justified by the problem, priorities and wider digital context. If you already know the specific service you need, that is a perfectly good starting point too.
